Tax Consultancy & NTN Registration

Tax rules change often. We keep you compliant with FBR and the provincial revenue authorities, and make sure every return is accurate and filed on time.

🧾 Tax consultancy

  • Income tax return filing
  • Sales tax: FBR, PRA and SRB
  • Withholding tax compliance
  • Replies to tax notices
  • IT exports and freelancer tax

🪪 NTN & Active Taxpayer List

  • NTN registration for individuals
  • NTN for companies, AOPs and firms
  • Active Taxpayer List (ATL) support
  • Renewals and status monitoring

Why the Active Taxpayer List matters

Being on the ATL affects the withholding tax rates applied on many transactions, such as banking, property and vehicle purchases. We help you file on time so your status stays active.
